Smart Search keeps its disposable search indexes and optional local model here.
Anki preserves user_files when the add-on is upgraded.

Profile indexes may contain local copies of searchable note/card text, field
names and values, tags, deck and note-type names, note/card identifiers, and
embeddings derived from note text. They stay on this computer and are not
synced by Smart Search. The add-on does not store review answers, review timing,
ease ratings, flags, suspension state, or scheduling history in these indexes.

With Anki closed, you may delete the profiles directory at any time; Smart
Search will rebuild it from Anki. The model and runtime directories are
optional local Semantic Search files and may also be deleted.
